Privacy Policy for Tap Manager FC Effective Date: May 1, 2026 Tap Manager FC is a mobile game developed by Travis Jones, located in Ohio, United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using Tap Manager FC, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 1. Information We Collect Tap Manager FC is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. The game does not require players to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or other sensitive personal information. The game may collect or process the following types of information: Local Game Data Tap Manager FC stores game progress and gameplay information on your device, such as your club name, team progress, match results, upgrades, rewards, settings, premium status, and other gameplay data. iCloud and CloudKit Data Tap Manager FC may use Apple iCloud and CloudKit to sync or back up game progress across your Apple devices. This may include gameplay data such as club progress, saved game state, premium status, settings, and other information needed to restore your game. This data is associated with your Apple iCloud account and is handled according to Apple’s iCloud and privacy practices. In-App Purchase Information Tap Manager FC includes in-app purchases, including a Premium Club one-time unlock. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment card information. Tap Manager FC uses RevenueCat to help manage purchases, verify premium access, and restore purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information, device identifiers, app identifiers, and technical information needed to provide purchase and entitlement services. Rewarded Ads Tap Manager FC includes optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. You may choose to watch an ad in exchange for in-game rewards, such as boosts. Watching rewarded ads is optional. You can play the game without choosing to watch rewarded ads. Google AdMob may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention information. This information may be used to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and provide rewards. Analytics and Usage Information Tap Manager FC may use analytics to understand how the game is used, improve gameplay balance, monitor premium purchase performance, and improve the app. RevenueCat may provide purchase and entitlement-related analytics, such as purchase status, premium access status, product interactions, and revenue-related events. Google AdMob may provide advertising-related analytics, such as ad impressions, ad interactions, rewarded ad completion, and ad performance. Technical Information The game and third-party services may collect technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, diagnostics, performance data, crash-related data if provided by the system, and general usage events. Precise Location Tap Manager FC does not collect precise location data. Personal Contact Information Tap Manager FC does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or address through the app. 2. How We Use Information We use information to: - Save and restore game progress - Sync game progress through iCloud or CloudKit - Provide gameplay features - Process and restore in-app purchases - Verify Premium Club access - Provide ad-free premium benefits where applicable - Provide optional rewarded ads - Deliver in-game rewards - Improve gameplay balance and app performance - Understand how players use the game - Measure ad and purchase performance - Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse - Comply with legal, platform, and App Store requirements 3. In-App Purchases and Premium Club Tap Manager FC offers a Premium Club one-time unlock. Premium Club may provide benefits such as removing ads, allowing ad-free access to rewarded boosts, and unlocking premium features or status described in the app. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information according to Apple’s own privacy policy and App Store terms. RevenueCat is used to help verify purchase status, manage premium access, and restore purchases. 4. Google AdMob may process information needed to show, measure, and verify ads. If you purchase Premium Club, ads may be removed or certain ad-based rewards may become available without watching ads, depending on the feature description in the app. 5. iCloud and CloudKit Tap Manager FC may use iCloud or CloudKit to store and sync game progress. You can manage iCloud settings through your Apple device settings. Disabling iCloud for the app may affect your ability to sync or restore game progress across devices. 6.
