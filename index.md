# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 4, 2026
**Last updated:** May 4, 2026

Tap Manager FC ("the app", "we", "us") is a tap-style football management
game. This policy explains what information the app handles, where that
information lives, and the third-party services involved.

In short: **the app does not show ads, does not track you across other apps
or websites, does not collect analytics or telemetry, and does not share
any personal information with advertisers or data brokers.**

---

## What we collect

We collect **one** category of information from you:

- **Purchase history.** When you buy the Boost Pass, Starter Pack, Manager
  Pack, or Premium Pass, the App Store sends a transaction receipt to our
  receipt-validation provider so the app can unlock what you paid for and
  restore purchases on your other Apple devices. The receipt is associated
  with an anonymous random identifier — not your name, email, Apple ID,
  device ID, or location.

That's the entire list. We do not collect your name, email address, phone
number, contacts, photos, location, advertising ID, device ID, browsing
history, or any biometric data. We do not run analytics or crash reporters.

---

## What we do not do

- **No ads.** The app does not display banner ads, interstitial ads,
  rewarded video ads, or any other form of advertising.
- **No tracking.** The app does not request App Tracking Transparency
  permission and does not use your Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  Nothing about your activity in the app is shared with advertisers,
  ad networks, attribution services, or third-party trackers.
- **No analytics.** No usage events, screen views, taps, sessions,
  or product-interaction data are sent off-device.
- **No crash or performance telemetry.** The app does not include a
  third-party crash reporter or performance monitor.
- **No selling data.** We do not sell, rent, or share your information
  with anyone for any commercial purpose.

---

## Where your data lives

- **Game progress** (your club, squad, facilities, season, cosmetics,
  trophies, daily goals) is saved locally on your device. If you have
  iCloud enabled, a copy is also stored in your **private iCloud
  CloudKit database**, which only your Apple ID can access. We do not
  have any access to that database. Apple's iCloud terms apply.

- **Purchase records** are processed by Apple and then mirrored to
  RevenueCat (our receipt-validation provider) for the sole purpose of
  determining what you have purchased and enabling Restore Purchases.
  See "Third-party services" below.

- **Game Center data** (player ID, achievements, leaderboards) is
  managed by Apple and governed by Apple's Game Center privacy terms.
  We do not store or transmit Game Center data ourselves.

---

## Third-party services

The app uses exactly one third-party service:

- **RevenueCat** — handles in-app purchase receipt validation and
  cross-device entitlement restore. RevenueCat is configured in
  anonymous mode, meaning your purchases are linked to a random
  identifier RevenueCat generates, not to your name, email, or
  Apple ID. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at
  https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

We do not use Google AdMob, Firebase, Meta SDK, analytics platforms,
attribution platforms, or any other third-party SDK.

## Children's privacy

The app is rated for ages 4+ and is suitable for all audiences. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the
sections above describe the only data the app handles, regardless of
the user's age. The app contains no user-to-user communication, no
chat, no third-party ads, and no data collection beyond purchase
records.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concern, please contact
us using the address below.

## Your rights

Because the app does not collect or store personal information that
identifies you, there is generally nothing for us to delete on
request. You can:

- **Reset local game progress** at any time via the app's
  Club tab → "Reset save". This permanently clears your local
  progress. (Paid purchases survive a reset so you don't lose what
  you paid for.)
- **Delete iCloud game data** via Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud →
  Manage Storage → Tap Manager FC → Delete Data.
- **Cancel or refund a purchase** through Apple's standard purchase
  history process at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you have a specific privacy request, contact us using the address
below and we will respond promptly.
